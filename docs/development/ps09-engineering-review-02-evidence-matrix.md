## Retained inputs and how to read a row

`C:line` means the exact local line in the preserved owner research catalog,
`docs/Policy-Sentinel-PNW-Research-and-Test-Case-Catalog-2026-09-05.md`,
raw SHA-256 `82c45788e51be6e127796fd6f2d0a2d48895b7f234e2ff3faf9ffc244979e152`.
The catalog contains dated research assertions and originating URL seeds; those
assertions are not reverified here. A local locator to that catalog is planning
provenance, not a citation to accepted source content. No URL has been invented,
followed or qualified by this matrix. A missing exact document URL requires
separately authorized bounded discovery, even when its institution is known.

[Run 2 candidate manifests](ps09-run-02-candidate-manifests.md), raw SHA-256

Access codes in the tables add concrete requirements to that full contract:
**I** = official instrument/agency metadata and source-specific excerpt rights;
**P** = party/first-party material, individual privacy and reuse review, no
automatic public display; **T** = unresolved Oregon access/terms boundary, in
addition to I or P; **X** = exact rendition/locator missing, so later discovery
needs its own finite authorization. Every row requires S/E/V/L; **N** flags the
applicable community-authority review boundary above. Public comments, personal contacts, court
service lists, claimant details, redaction inference, sensitive cultural content,
maps, geometry and land-interest determinations are excluded. County eligibility
requires the official county record to explicitly name the Nation. State/federal
records without exact Nation evidence remain `general_jurisdiction`.
