## Shared slot and evidence contract

Each numbered slot is a finite discovery requirement. A slot family can later
contain multiple separately identified documents; it cannot merge speakers,
proceedings, renditions or versions. For **every real slot**, accepted source
ID, official document ID, issuer, speaker, proceeding ID, version, digest,
issue/retrieval/update/as-of/validity dates, exact citation, current status,
reuse permission and refresh evidence remain **unknown/unaccepted**. A title
or candidate docket spelling does not fill any of those accepted fields.

The intended originating role in the tables is a requirement to verify. Agency
hosting does not make a party filing an agency finding. A proposal, scheduled
filing, completed document and closed proceeding have separate meanings. No
future source body may be generated. Missing, unknown, unavailable, outside
coverage, not observed, not assessed, disputed and rejected remain distinct;
none establishes absence, falsity or currentness.

Real citation acceptance later requires the exact originating issuer and
speaker, document/version, official URL, rendition role, reviewed digest and
locator, with claim-specific evidence and use rights. The local fixture uses
declared synthetic `.invalid` references and digest strings only; exact replay
proves graph consistency, not originating bytes, CAS custody, human review,
official-text reproduction rights or current facts.

Only public synthetic fixture data enters the executable boundary. Real
excerpt, export and public-display permissions remain unknown. No personal
contacts, court service lists, claimant details, source bodies, redaction
inferences, coordinates, geometry or land interests are authorized. Geography
below selects planning contexts; it cannot create a Nation association, rights,
jurisdiction, eligibility or consent conclusion. Unmapped retained records
remain Unclassified under the unchanged taxonomy contract.

Refresh requirements refer to the later canonical lifecycle, not a new store:
changed evidence creates an immutable version; older evidence keeps its dates;
an unavailable source exposes a gap. Only a separately approved, compatible,
checksum-validated shard from the same adapter's last approved output artifact
may supply dated stale public material.
