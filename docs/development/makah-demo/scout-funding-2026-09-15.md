## Proposed feasibility treatment

Non-federal funding pages are heterogeneous human-facing sites with no
machine contract, no stated reuse terms and program cadences that change
without notice. The proposed treatment is one reviewed family row in
`docs/source-feasibility.md` ("NGO, foundation and intermediary funding
programs") rather than a row per funder: metadata-and-link only, build-time
only, no scraping, program eligibility preserved as source language, and every
program remaining `general_jurisdiction` and `Unclassified`. Priority and
status: reviewed, not admitted; gate `G-J` for any later per-funder contract,
and `G-F` if any funder imposes access or licensing terms.
