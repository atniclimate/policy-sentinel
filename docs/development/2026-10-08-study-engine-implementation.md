   active response deadline.
2. Aggregate consultation statements do not imply individual Nation participation.
3. Empty searches identify collections, capability, coverage and date scope.
4. Intertribal positions remain attributable to the issuing organization.
5. Authoritative geography and jurisdiction relationships require explicit evidence.
6. State-federal dependencies require documented statutory/procedural evidence.
7. Common-origin copies do not count as independent corroboration.
8. Environmental metrics preserve alternatives, units, baselines, scales and qualifications.
9. Refined search retains evidenced parent proceeding/docket context separately
   from direct matches.
10. Exports retain immutable evidence identity and distinguish analyst content;
    public exports exclude private notes and restricted derivatives.
