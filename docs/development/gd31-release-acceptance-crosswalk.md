50,000,000,000 bytes. GD-31 represents successor contracts and finite manifests;
later dispatch requires their actual source, access, reuse, custody and capacity
conditions. Historical zero-budget or consumed run gates are not repurposed.
The total includes originals, derived data, metadata, indexes, outputs,
cold/historical custody and peak temporary space; independent run ceilings and
post-write disk floors still apply. A planning ceiling is not a reservation or
a successful preflight. No new general-engine public delivery grant follows.
The demo's D-083/D-084 publication/runtime authority remains demo-specific.

Historical source-specific gate objects remain frozen in their recorded states.
A future exact owner authorization for a currently ungranted credential, terms
or access operation must be represented by a reviewed successor gate/schema
change and linked to the affected current item. Source qualification,
preparation conformance and D-086's general acquisition direction cannot
discharge that authorization. Identity evidence conditions likewise require an
explicit reviewed successor representation rather than rewriting
`G-BIA-IDENTITY`. References in current acceptance to resolving these conditions
mean satisfying their actual authority/evidence requirements through that
successor representation, never modifying the historical grant or substituting

speaker separately; stable entity and document/version identities; exact URL

## Final acceptance record and exclusions

GD-27's eventual evidence must identify the exact package/build and constituent
versions, source/corpus/receipt and citation digests, completed Part B evidence,
all four observed demonstrations, and the GD-53 claim dispositions. It must
retain first failures, denominators, omissions, test skips, source gaps and
repair/retest evidence, with no unresolved mandatory blocking finding.
Source refresh/failure tests must use only checksum-validated prior approved
output of the same adapter, preserve its original as-of date and label it
stale/degraded; without one, omit the source and mark unavailable. This is a
failure contract, not permission for continuous refresh.

Part C remains outside this completion claim: continuous monitoring/refresh,
notifications, public/Pages delivery, push services, optional AI summaries,
Canadian wiring, remote publication, nationwide county custody and full text
for every discovery entry. Paid/contact, credentials, access-triggered terms,
private/shared data, and external exchanges retain their actual grants and
agreements. The public application remains usable without AI. No general
legal-effect, jurisdiction, eligibility, rights, comprehensive coverage or
community endorsement claim follows from successful packaging or acceptance.
