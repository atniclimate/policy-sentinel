# Source feasibility

Status: Phase A research, updated 2026-07-31. This is an implementation decision record, not a representation that any adapter or dataset already exists.

## Evaluation rules

- Originating agencies, legislatures, courts, and Tribal governments are the authority. [Data.gov](https://data.gov/about/) is discovery metadata only when an originating source exists.
- Every public adapter runs at build time on the weekly or manual workflow. The public site must not depend on source CORS, expose an API key, or call a provider at runtime.
- A source-explicit Nation association requires exact evidence in an official record. Official subject labels may map deterministically to the taxonomy; missing or ambiguous labels produce `Unclassified`, never a keyword or AI classification.
- A refresh is staged and schema-, count-, and provenance-validated before release. On failure, retain that source's last-known-good artifact and publish its degraded health and actual data-as-of date. If no prior artifact exists, omit the source and mark it unavailable.
- “No published limit” means no numeric limit was found in the cited primary documentation. It does not authorize aggressive collection. Adapters must identify themselves where requested, cache responsibly, paginate, back off on `429`/`503`, and obey later provider changes.

## Priority sequence

1. Validate the 575-Nation registry from the annual BIA notice and establish stable internal IDs. Resolve the Nation-to-state coverage crosswalk before public release.
2. Build Federal Register as the first no-key federal policy adapter. Keep
   Grants.gov unavailable until G-B-GRANTS approves the exact current terms and
   bounded build-time canaries resolve its published contract gaps.
3. Complete credential-free Congress.gov, GovInfo, and Regulations.gov contract fixtures, while keeping each live adapter behind its own source-scoped key gate.
4. Add Washington LWS, Washington rule/executive sources, and a small reviewed GOIA accord registry.
5. Add official court and IBIA sources plus source-verified landmark records in independent increments.
6. Add Oregon rule/executive sources independently; keep live Oregon OData behind its terms and credential gate.
7. Keep Idaho legislative automation blocked until an official structured path is validated; Idaho administrative rules may proceed independently.
8. Pilot source-reviewed Tribal-government, county, treaty, compact, and other long-tail registries.

## Stop gates and unresolved facts

- **Nation-state crosswalk:** the annual recognition notice establishes recognition, not a reliable beta-state assignment. A primary or reviewed source set must support the WA/OR/ID coverage notice. TLD geometry and addresses are not acceptable evidence.
- **Grants.gov terms and contract:** API access or use accepts the current
  provider terms, and the sample-only documentation leaves pagination,
  nullability, deadline/time-zone, and canonical-URL behavior unresolved. Make
  no request or XML-body retrieval and implement no substitute contract until
  the owner approves G-B-GRANTS for the exact then-current terms and build-time
  operation.
- **Oregon agreement:** no acceptance, credential acquisition, live OData request, or live OData adapter until the owner approves the official Acceptable Use Agreement and proposed secret-handling workflow. Credential-free contract design, synthetic fixtures, and independent official Oregon rule/executive work may proceed.
- **Idaho legislation:** no production scraping. Proceed only after a stable official structured source or owner-authorized provider clarification.
- **Court completeness:** no source identified provides a comprehensive, primary, no-cost corpus across federal, Washington, Oregon, and Idaho courts. Coverage must stay court- and date-specific.
- **Text rights:** official availability is not blanket permission to redistribute entire works. Every adapter needs a documented field/excerpt policy before public artifacts are generated.
- **Registrations and contact:** Congress.gov, GovInfo, Regulations.gov, and possibly NARA require later approved keys. No account registration, terms acceptance, or third-party contact occurred in Phase A.
