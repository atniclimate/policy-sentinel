# Boldt decision official-source gap review

Accessed: 2026-07-31

Implementation state: documented source gap; no source registry entry, adapter,
record, copied text, Nation association, or public artifact output

External authorization: none requested or granted

## Reviewed primary sources

- [Western District of Washington special case notices](https://www.wawd.uscourts.gov/special-case-notices)
- [Western District of Washington pre-CM/ECF docket entries 1-4888](https://www.wawd.uscourts.gov/sites/wawd/files/70-9213batch1.pdf)
- [Later Western District of Washington findings and memorandum](https://www.wawd.uscourts.gov/sites/wawd/files/Makah09-01FFCLandMemorandum.pdf)
- [Official U.S. Reports decision at 443 U.S. 658](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USREPORTS-443/pdf/USREPORTS-443-658.pdf)
- [Supreme Court bound-volume index](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/boundvolumes.aspx)

The Western District's special-case page is the originating court's current
official case surface. It identifies civil case `C70-9213-RSM`, describes the
litigation commonly called the Boldt Fishing Case, and links official
pre-CM/ECF docket sheets. The first docket PDF covers entries 1-4888 from
1970-09-18 through 1978-08-31. Its 1974-02-12 entry records the filing and entry
of Final Decision #I with findings of fact and conclusions of law. The docket
sheet proves an event and document identity; it is not the body of the decision.

The later district-court memorandum and the official 1979 U.S. Reports decision
provide primary judicial context and cite the earlier district decision. They
are later decisions, not substitutes for the originating 1974 decision text.
The reporter citation retained for research is *United States v. Washington*,
384 F. Supp. 312 (W.D. Wash. 1974).

## Exact gap

No originating-court online copy of the 1974 decision body was located on the
reviewed Western District special-case page or its linked pre-CM/ECF docket
materials. The reviewed docket link exposes an index, not a decision document.
The later district and Supreme Court decisions cannot be relabeled as the 1974
decision or used to fill its missing source-text and reproduction evidence.

The source-specific reproduction basis for an online 1974 copy therefore
remains unverified. Policy Sentinel must not use commercial reporter text,
third-party transcriptions, library copies, later opinions, or search-result
snippets as a replacement. No full text, excerpt, summary, or derived holding
is approved.

## Landmark and Nation decision

The verified court identity, filing date, reporter citation, and later official
context are not enough to emit a detailed landmark record. A record still needs
an exact originating official decision URL or another expressly reviewed
official reproduction path, a complete source-use decision, versioned source
configuration, adapter/provenance tests, and the source-specific G-J evidence
decision.

No Nation association is created. Names, parties, treaty context, geography,
later case descriptions, or the project's historical understanding cannot
substitute for exact association evidence in an official source. The gap is
metadata-and-link research only and produces no source health or
last-known-good shard.

## Recheck trigger

Reopen this gap only if the originating court or another authoritative federal
publisher exposes the actual 1974 decision through a stable official URL with a
reviewable reproduction basis. A later official decision can remain contextual
evidence but cannot satisfy that trigger by itself.
