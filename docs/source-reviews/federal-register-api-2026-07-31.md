# Federal Register

The Office of the Federal Register publishes agency rules, proposed rules and notices. Results identify the issuing agency and document number. A source abstract may appear in bounded form. For the official edition, follow the document's GovInfo link.

Material that appears in regular or special editions may be reproduced under 1 CFR 2.6. Linked attachments, comments and incorporated standards require separate treatment. A generated search snippet does not establish what appeared in the edition. [Federal Register API](https://www.federalregister.gov/developers/documentation/api/v1) and [1 CFR 2.6](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-1/chapter-I/part-2/section-2.6).
