Scope: documentation-only preparation under D-086/D-087 in the
[development-plan revision](../../decisions/2026-10-06-development-plan-revision.md).

## Public projection and fixture requirements

A future source contract must select fields individually, with source ID, exact
field locator, official URL, retrieval time, source update time when supplied,
transform/schema version and validation state. No field below is newly observed
in a provider response today.

| Family | Minimum proposed public projection | Required representative and adverse fixtures |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Federal Register | Document number, exact non-personal title, source type/agency, citation, separately typed dates, official links and explicit correction references; exact official subject labels only | Missing agency/date, historic absent rendition, correction/withdrawal as later record, duplicate/reordered pages, truncated responses, unsafe links, contact-bearing abstract, empty valid issue and source failure |
| GovInfo | Package and optional granule IDs, collection, originating body, reviewed non-personal title, issue/modified dates, source version, advertised official links and separate local digest | Missing optional format, 1994 FR exception, duplicate/cursor loop, parent-ID mismatch, HTTP-200 error page, generated-file 503, mismatched fixity, reprocessed bytes, PII-bearing title/MODS and incomplete court coverage |

| Congress.gov | Congress/type/number, exact title, chamber, source actions and dates, explicit law relationship, text-version links, scheme-qualified subject labels | Historical missing subjects/actions, malformed IDs/URLs, duplicate/reordered/truncated pages, text-only update, missing optional fields, member contact/depiction fields rejected, unknown envelope and auth/rate failure |
| Regulations.gov | Opaque governmental document/docket IDs, agency/type/subtype labels, reviewed non-personal title, typed dates/status labels and stable detail links | Unsafe shared comment fields, supporting-material exclusion, reassignment/replacement, same-second update ties, >5,000-result window, duplicate pages, absent optional data, truncated response, 429 and unresolved attachment rights |

All projections exclude credentials and keyed URLs, headers/cookies, raw
responses/errors, personal/contact fields, comments, signatures, unrestricted
free text, maps/parcel/land details, culturally restricted material and unrelated
extensions. Publication eligibility must be checked even for metadata titles.
Unknown fields are not silently carried through. Public artifacts receive no
raw corpus.

No reviewed federal family automatically provides a Nation association.
Require exact official evidence and its URL plus accepted identity resolution;
otherwise preserve `general_jurisdiction`. Exact official subjects need a
versioned deterministic mapping; absent/unmapped values remain
`Unclassified`. Agency, title keywords, geography, sponsors, regulatory
eligibility and a broad Indigenous subject label do not satisfy either rule.

Keep source/route health independent. A failed refresh can reuse only the same
adapter's checksum-validated approved shard with its original data-as-of and a
stale/degraded label. Without one, omit records and report unavailable. Do not
manufacture a healthy state from a successful documentation read. Existing
source-level health cannot silently represent per-collection GovInfo or
per-agency Regulations.gov shards; a successor must version that dimension or
conservatively degrade the whole source. Documented historic absence is outside
coverage; an unavailable attempted rendition is a failure; uninspected history
is unknown, not proof of absence.

## Contract-preparation shortlist

Select **GovInfo direct government-authored Federal Register instruments** as
the federal candidate for the initial pilot. This is a shortlist for preparing a
bounded successor contract, with **activation evidence required**. The newly
dispatch-ready pilot subset is empty. The rationale is the current official direct-distribution
and reproduction evidence plus the retained narrow parser/custody experience.
It does not complete the GovInfo or Federal Register API requirement.

GD-31 should name fresh targets and operation IDs, restrict the pilot to at
most three individually reviewed FR instruments within a proposed 2025–2026
selection window, and freeze exact advertised/documented URLs before dispatch.
This is a planning bound, not a selected build range. No target, current policy
status or complete topic population is asserted here. Exclude public inspection,
comments, attachments, unrelated linked content, private inserts and sensitive
location/contact blocks. Never dispatch a consumed historical operation.

Before acquisition, resolve current robots/host conditions, identify each
instrument and rendition, complete capture/analysis/display/excerpt/export
decisions separately, and bind the approved external custody/output paths.
Transport request, byte, time, concurrency and retry ceilings are intentionally
unset here; GD-31 must freeze them in a reviewed successor manifest before any
dispatch. GD-18 must measure retained objects, derivatives and output against
the adopted 50 GB total managed footprint. This finite selection proposal is
neither an operation grant nor a measured storage report.

No new acquisition may use the expired September profile. A current successor
contract and its validation/manifest must be represented under GD-31 before
dispatch under the adopted D-086 direction. Registration, provider-term
acceptance, private-data use, external contact and publication remain separate
closed operations. Item admission, meaningful extraction, offline-workflow
usability and authorized output acceptance must be evidenced after capture;
none is supplied by this survey.
