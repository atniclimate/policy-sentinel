# Initial regional source matrix

Reviewed: 2026-10-07. Scope: GD-17 documentation-only source discovery for
Washington, Oregon, Idaho, Alaska, California, Montana, and Nevada.

This is a source-selection and gap record, not an acquisition manifest or source
admission. No policy body, API record, PDF, raw response, credential, or provider
profile was acquired or retained. No existing source was enabled or renewed.
There are **zero newly qualified dispatch-ready integrated sources** and no
text-output acceptance in this review.

The [development-plan revision](../../decisions/2026-10-06-development-plan-revision.md)
(D-086/D-087) controls planning scope. Named Alaska, California, Montana, and
Nevada communities guide selection priorities; they do not establish boundaries,
membership, county association, or Nation relationships. Local governmental
instruments may be discovered as `general_jurisdiction` without naming a Nation.
No locality-to-community manifest is asserted here.

## Evidence and common limits

- **Direct 10-07:** the originating documentation or index was readable during
  this review. This is not a record-contract canary.
- **Indexed 10-07:** an official-site search result supplied discovery evidence;
  its underlying page or current content was not fully verified.
- **Retained date:** the linked repository review supplies historical evidence,
  not a current revalidation. Known access-acceptance gates were not revisited.
- **Unavailable 10-07:** the research tool could not retrieve the page. This is
  a transport observation, not proof of source denial, outage, or nonexistence.

All matrix cells are **evidence blocked** for a new integrated source unless
explicitly marked **terms/credential blocked**. Previously implemented disabled
adapters remain as recorded in the registry; this survey changes no status.
Historical sampled dates are not completeness guarantees. Documented range,
later selected build range, and emitted range are distinct: **no build range is
selected and no records are emitted here**. Pre-1980 non-landmark records remain
metadata-and-link candidates.

Unless a cell states otherwise, numeric rate/quota, machine pagination and
terminal-page rules, formal schema, revision relationships, retry behavior,
robots/bulk-use policy, reproduction/excerpt permission, current attribution
requirements, and exact historical completeness are **unknown or unqualified**.
A readable public page needed no credential for that documentation observation;
this says nothing about a linked API or export. No provider CORS contract was
tested. Future retrieval is build-time only, never a browser dependency.
Publication cadence below describes the publisher, not an approved product
refresh schedule. No product request/byte/time budget is created by this survey.

Official visibility does not grant reuse. Copyright notices, government-edicts
reasoning, publisher contracts, third-party material and metadata rights require
source-specific review. The following is a **proposed minimization allowlist,
not a license or admission**: exact public instrument identifier, privacy-safe
title, originating government, explicit jurisdiction and instrument type,
source-labeled dates/status/version, official canonical locator, and field-level
provenance/retrieval/update/validation data. Exclude raw bodies, captions naming
private people, personal/contact fields, comments, credentials, attachments,
parcel/land/cultural information, private research and unrelated content.
Unmapped taxonomy remains `Unclassified`; no keyword or geographic inference
supplies a Nation association or legal effect.

## Narrow pilot contract-preparation option

The strongest regional successor-preparation candidate is a small Washington
official **bill-version/session-law** slice using the declared official index and
its advertised rendition links. This is a recommendation to prepare a reviewed
contract, **not** a finding of dispatch readiness, new body access, or permission
to emit text.

The [2026-09-05 direct-source review](../real-policy-direct-2026-09-05.md)
distinguished official legislative text from RCW/WAC compilations, annotations
and third-party material. Its government-edicts conclusion was a narrow review
inference for the selected official text, not a blanket publisher license.
Historical profiles expired on 2026-10-05 and the historical operation grant is
spent. Current directory/service/disclaimer observations do not renew either.

A successor must establish fresh exact policies and an allowed route, finite
session/identifier/rendition manifest, request/byte/time ceilings, custody and
output boundaries, provenance, privacy projection, lifecycle/partial-veto and
version reconciliation, and source-isolated health before dispatch. ID/variant/
chapter/year/session checks must bind to official evidence; file timestamps do
not become effective dates. RCW/WAC compilation and public text redistribution
remain outside this proposed pilot unless separately reviewed and admitted.

California Downloadable Data Files are a secondary **evidence-blocked research
lead** until current official documentation is readable and verified. No
California adapter viability claim follows from the retired site's pointer.
No single originating seven-state API was qualified in this bounded survey;
that is not a claim that none exists. Federal sources with regional applicability
need their own authority and record-level relevance review.

Before any candidate admission, fixtures must test representative/missing/
malformed identities and dates, duplicates, reordered/truncated pagination,
mutable versions, privacy-bearing titles, stale material and failures. New
sources have no last-known-good shard: failure means omission/unavailable.
Only a checksum-validated shard from that same adapter's approved prior output
could later be reused, preserving its original data-as-of time.
