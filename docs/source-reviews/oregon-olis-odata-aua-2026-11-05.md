# Oregon Legislature

Oregon legislative search is unavailable in the demo. ATNI Climate accepted the Oregon Legislature Open Data Service agreement on November 5, 2026. Integration still awaits its first checked request.

Oregon Legislature Information Services requires clients to retain associated metadata and use the service in preference to regular website scraping. The agreement leaves text reproduction unsettled. The demo displays no Oregon summaries or bill text. [Oregon legislative data](https://www.oregonlegislature.gov/citizen_engagement/Pages/data.aspx) and [acceptable use agreement](https://www.oregonlegislature.gov/citizen_engagement/Documents/OLODataAcceptableUseAgreement.pdf).
