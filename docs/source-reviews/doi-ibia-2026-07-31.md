IBIA is the originating Department of the Interior appellate body. DOI says it
exercises delegated authority to issue final Department decisions in appeals
involving Indian matters and is institutionally separate from BIA. Departmental
finality does not prove that a disposition was a merits opinion, remains
unmodified, or was not later reviewed by a federal district court.

## Declared and observed range

DOI directs users to the `IBIA Decisions (1970-Present)` search database. The
chronological index links annual pages from 1970 through 2026. The earliest
displayed 1970 entry is dated 1970-08-13. Pre-1970 probate appellate decisions
are a separate, incomplete physical collection that DOI describes as not
readily available.

The annual index is not a current-completeness inventory. DOI expressly warns
that it may not always be as current as the separate decision-search database.
The search application has no documented API, response schema, pagination,
count, stable query contract, quota, or service guarantee. No complete range
claim can be built from the year links.

Bounded aggregate checks of selected annual pages found 8 linked rows in 1970,
30 in 1980, 126 in 1990, 113 in 2000, 143 link occurrences but 142 distinct
targets in 2010, 56 in 2025, and 38 in 2026. These counts are dated structural
observations only, not evidence of completeness or stable totals.

## Fields and status semantics

The exact annual table supplies only:

- displayed case caption;
- `Date Decided`;
- displayed IBIA reporter citation; and
- a linked official decision document.

It does not supply docket number, exact document or disposition label,
opinion-versus-order form, affirmance without opinion, precedential status,
reconsideration or supersession relationships, or later judicial-review state.
Those fields cannot be inferred from a generic `Cases Decided` heading.

Two individually reviewed official document headers demonstrate the gap:

- `72 IBIA 63` is docket `IBIA 26-029` and is labeled
  `Order Docketing and Dismissing Appeal`.
- `1 IBIA 56` is docket `IBIA 70-2 Supp.`, is labeled
  `Order Denying Petition for Rehearing`, and expressly relates to
  `1 IBIA 1`.

Current regulations separately address written decisions, affirmance without
opinion, dismissal, finality, and reconsideration. A caption is not a headnote
or summary. A citation identifies one reported item, while a docket can
identify the proceeding; the two must not be concatenated into the current
single identifier field.

## Current-year structural observation

One exact aggregate-only request to the 2026 page returned exact-URL HTTP 200
UTF-8 HTML with no content encoding or declared length: 70,119 bytes in seven
nonempty chunks. Inert `parse5` inspection counted 1,816 nodes, 979 attributes,
maximum depth 20, 27,011 text code units, one table, and no parse errors. No
response body was written or retained.

The unique three-column table had one exact header and 38 rows dated from
2026-01-15 through 2026-07-28. It contained 35 unique caption hashes, two
duplicate-caption groups, ten captions containing the source phrase
`Estate of`, and 29 versus-style captions. All 38 rows had one distinct
citation and one `www.oha.doi.gov` link. Thirty-seven citations matched the
reviewed IBIA pattern; one displayed `72 IBA 023`. The linked document header
uses `72 IBIA 23`. The displayed date order had four descending-order
violations.

Separate bounded review found that the 2010 page displays `52 IBIA 327` with
the same target used for `52 IBIA 337`, and includes a literal `Blank Page`
row. Source spelling, captions, and citations must be preserved as displayed
with validation state; defects must never be silently repaired from a guessed
pattern.

## Access and failure behavior

The DOI index pages are public, unauthenticated HTML. DOI publishes no numeric
limit and its robots file specifies `Crawl-delay: 1`. HTML index responses
supplied neither a useful provider checksum nor a documented update schedule.
DOI's general disclaimer disclaims completeness and timeliness.

Every observed decision link and the separate search application are on
`www.oha.doi.gov`. That host's exact robots file is:

```text
User-agent: *
Disallow: /
```

An exact-citation search request was rejected with HTTP 403. No retry,
workaround, browser automation, user-agent rotation, or evasion was attempted.
Policy Sentinel must not automate the OHA search application or decision
documents.

The index alone cannot validate the docket, item form, disposition, text
identity, revisions, or relationships required by B6. A source refresh cannot
claim current collection health when DOI says the index can lag the search
database.

## Privacy, land, and Nation evidence

IBIA jurisdiction includes probate and heirship, trust-land use and
acquisition, minerals, rights-of-way, trespass, encumbrances, and disputes over
recognition of Tribal officials. Annual captions include estates, decedents,
minors, and other natural persons. Reviewed document bodies contained
personal, allotment, parcel, ownership, mineral, acreage, signature, and
governance-dispute detail.

The public product must retain none of that content. Even an
institution-only caption cannot make its body safe. Judge names and signatures
are not Nation evidence. A Nation-like name in a caption does not establish a
validated current Nation identity, endorsement, outcome, governing-body
legitimacy, rights, jurisdiction, land interest, or relevance to another
party. Geography, BIA region, subject matter, individuals, and cited cases are
never association evidence. While G-BIA-IDENTITY remains unresolved, all
possible records have zero Nation associations.

## Reproduction and attribution

DOI says federal-agency materials are generally public domain but does not
guarantee the status of a specific item. It asks users to link to DOI rather
than mirror portions of the site and separately protects logos and trademarks.
No IBIA-specific reuse license, excerpt rule, or required wording was found.

The only reviewed publication boundary is:

- attribution to
  `U.S. Department of the Interior, Office of Hearings and Appeals, Interior Board of Indian Appeals`;
- exact, allowlisted metadata and official links only;
- no PDF, excerpt, summary, signature, headnote, or source corpus;
- no personal, probate, land, mineral, ownership, contact, or governance
  detail;
- `Unclassified` absent an exact mapped official subject; and
- zero Nation associations.

DOI's privacy policy says visits can record network, device, referrer, page,
and search information. Any future access remains build-time only, with no
cookies, credentials, referrer, user input, or public browser call.

## Decision

Source-registry 1.11 records `doi-ibia-decisions` truthfully as a disabled
`official_index` source with `adapter: null`, observed coverage beginning
1970-08-13, metadata-and-links reproduction, no subject mappings, and a
source-specific health boundary.

Broad adapter work is evidence-blocked. It can resume only if DOI/OHA provides
a documented crawlable interface or explicit permission for the decision
documents and the repository has:

1. separate citation, docket, decision-date, exact document-form,
   disposition, publication-state, and revision-history fields;
2. a deterministic privacy-safe selection contract that excludes every
   personal, probate, land, mineral, ownership, and governance-dispute field;
3. exact index-to-document identity reconciliation and defect handling;
4. no collection-completeness claim based on the lagging index;
5. exact official Nation identity reconciliation before any association; and
6. source-specific synthetic, provenance, health, and last-known-good tests.

No adapter or parser scaffold is created for a source whose controlling
evidence cannot be retrieved within its published access policy.
