# GovInfo

The U.S. Government Publishing Office provides records from multiple collections. Cite the collection and package or granule identifier together with the issuing agency, legislature or court.

Policy Sentinel displays metadata and official links. Publication by GPO alone does not settle the rights to reproduce every part of a publication. Source summaries, exhibits and illustrations require item-specific review. [GovInfo Developer Hub](https://www.govinfo.gov/developers) and [GPO policies](https://www.govinfo.gov/about/policies).
