# Local workbench source evidence

The local workbench lists official evidence locations alongside the dates and
outcomes recorded in earlier reviews. An official page may have changed since
the date shown. The catalog is separate from sources searched by the public demo
and from collections retained in a specific local corpus.

The October 7, 2026 discovery review covered federal, state and intertribal
source leads. It recorded candidate interfaces, documented coverage and open
questions about access, reuse and retrieval. The seven-state interface register
records an observation date for each official link. Its Oregon entry uses
documentation observed on July 31, 2026.

The GovInfo, Washington legislative-text and federal court direct-document
profiles retain the dates and official evidence links from their September 5,
2026 review. That review expired on October 5, 2026. These profiles support
replay of previously reviewed local material. New retrieval requires its own
source review and authorization.

The earlier working review notes and operational records are held separately.
This page records their relationship to the public catalog. For the demo's
source and reuse notes, see
[Source coverage and reuse](https://github.com/atniclimate/policy-sentinel/blob/main/reference/sources.md).
