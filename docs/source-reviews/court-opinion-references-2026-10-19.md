# Federal court opinion references

GovInfo supplies links and metadata for its U.S. Courts Opinions and United States Reports collections. Cite GovInfo and the issuing court. The collections have different coverage, and an empty search does not establish the absence of an opinion.

The panel links to the official publication and copies no opinion text. A government collection may include third-party copyrighted material. CourtListener search links open a separate service. [U.S. Courts Opinions](https://www.govinfo.gov/help/uscourts), [United States Reports](https://www.govinfo.gov/help/usreports) and [GPO policies](https://www.govinfo.gov/about/policies).
