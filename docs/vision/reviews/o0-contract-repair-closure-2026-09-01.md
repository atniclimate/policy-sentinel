## Coherence decision before candidate editing

All 18 repairs fit one contract: a fixture-registry-closed deterministic state
machine with one run-global custody profile, per-source semantic identity,
globally durable content-addressed receipts, exact process-crash recovery, and
an explicitly narrowed Windows threat model. The design does not need a native
bridge, new dependency, schema, fixture, runtime implementation, product import,
or external operation. Governance changes remain limited to durable additive
history and convergence isolation. Candidate editing may therefore proceed
within the five authorized paths.
